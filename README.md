### Hi, I'm **Lucio Carvalho Jr** 👋

Cloud Architect & SRE based in **Québec, Canada** 🇨🇦 — originally from São Paulo, Brazil 🇧🇷, with a chapter in Toulouse, France 🇫🇷.

Over 15 years I've designed infrastructure across Brazil, France, and Canada, worked with payment security (PCI-DSS), migrated monoliths to microservices, and built CI/CD pipelines that actually work.

I write about DevOps, SRE, and platform engineering in Portuguese at **[capivaracode.com](https://capivaracode.com)**.
